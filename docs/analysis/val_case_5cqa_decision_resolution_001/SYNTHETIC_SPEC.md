# Source-independent verification design

Ordinary tests use constructed records, never private data or frozen models.

- Unequal masses .001/.003 kg, q=.001 kg/kg, retained predictions 2/0 mg/g:
  residuals +1/-1, weights 1/4 and 3/4, R=1, B=-.5, V=.75. Unequal
  allowances verify kg-to-mg/g conversion and L2/L1 propagation separately.
- Constant residuals: V=0; weighted-zero mean: B=0; opposing shot biases:
  mean B=0 but mean absB>0. Never pool errors or square aggregated means.
- Synthetic two/four-condition triples verify nested means and paired deletion;
  [1,2,3],[4,5,6] after deleting 1 yields 3.75, not pooled 4.0. Verify the
  independent influence identity for net gain and both contributions.
- R²=B²+V and contribution-sum identities; positive/adverse opposing terms;
  material negative V rejected, negligible subtraction roundoff documented.
- B intervals crossing zero, signed numerator divided by positive intervals,
  outward endpoints compared to exact rational arithmetic, V relaxation and
  nonnegative intersection, zero point denominator and unresolved zero-containing
  denominator bounds.
- Inclusive upper limits versus strict condition wins; conjunction precedence;
  relative rule candidate-.85*reference even at zero reference (not caffeine's
  forced FAIL); ratio guards independent from component decisions.
- Incomplete C07 retains all six flow shots; complete-shot failure witnesses
  divided by original three, with missing full points never filled from subset.
- Reject missing, duplicate, wrong-condition, arm/panel mismatch, wrong species,
  coordinate/support mismatch, nonfinite and negative-allowance records.
- Synthetic admission chain uses actual nested completion files schema, STARTED
  receipt/COMPLETE completion, explicit case-sensitive public fallback filenames,
  no fallback for present mismatch, optional failures without needless core block.
- Public projection includes only baseline aggregates and exhaustive family
  ranges/counts; no per-deletion vector, membership or per-condition extrema.
- Helper finite-sensitivity records do not resolve population uncertainty.
  Historical allowance scope covers retained numerical allowances only.

Integration execution compares every retained historical aggregate and decision,
direct optional fractions, all eighteen paired deletion identities, all scenario
contribution sums and flag totals. Record numeric maximum discrepancies and every
decision discrepancy. Tolerance is arithmetic-only as declared in PROTOCOL.
