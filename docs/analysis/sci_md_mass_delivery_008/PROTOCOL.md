## Decisions and numerical bounds

For each ordered pair, e is predicted minus observed TDS in percentage points:
R_ji=sqrt(sum d*e^2/sum d); B_ji=sum d*e/sum d.
Make bias absolute WITHIN each pair. Compute pair metrics first, never average
predictions. Each calibration row averages its N-1 target pairs equally; each
target column averages its N-1 calibrators equally. Overall summaries average
all ordered pairs equally. Retain rows/columns privately; publish rights-safe
aggregate distributions, including adverse calibration-choice sensitivity.

Pair adequacy: R<=1.00 pp AND |B|<=0.50 pp. A calibration choice passes if
mean R<=1.00, mean |B|<=0.50 and >=ceil(.75*(N-1)) target pairs pass.
Arm adequacy requires balanced R<=1.00, balanced mean |B|<=0.50 and
>=ceil(.75*N) calibration choices pass. N=11 requires 8/10 and 9/11 respectively.
These are task working budgets, not assay uncertainty, significance or universal
espresso quality standards. Never relax thresholds after outcomes.

I_CAL=A2 versus F2, I_CHEM=A2 versus A0, I_LOCAL=A2 versus M are separate.
Each requires ALL: >=0.10 pp balanced-R reduction, >=15% relative reduction,
>=ceil(.75*N) definite target-column mean-R wins, >=ceil(.75*N) definite
calibration-row mean-R wins, balanced mean-|B| deterioration<=0.10 pp.
Propagate interval numerical allowances into weighted-norm R bounds and
weighted-absolute B bounds, nonnegative |B| bounds, equal-pair means,
differences, relative gains and definite/possible win counts. A definite win
requires candidate upper below comparator lower. Reference lower<=1e-12 pp
cannot manufacture relative gain. Threshold straddles remain unresolved.
No confidence interval or independence is inferred from repeated scores.

Full positive decisions require complete declared support. Known nonnegative
partial squared-error lower bounds use FULL declared pair mass and full pair
counts; they may prove failure. Partial signed errors cannot establish full
absolute-bias failure because missing errors may cancel. Unsupported outcomes
