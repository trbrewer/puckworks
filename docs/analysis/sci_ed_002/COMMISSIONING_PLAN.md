# Commissioning and cost plan

The model separates procurement, roasting, splitting, moisture, total content, reference extraction, standards/QC, bridge, homogeneity/stability, logistics, QA, optional espresso, sealed VG06, and contingency. All values are USD planning ranges dated 2026-08-26; they are engineering assumptions, not vendor quotations. Recalculate subtotals directly from CSV before an authorization decision. The paired-analyte preparation denominator is 108 primary total-content preparations plus 144 bridge preparations; costs per primary unit and participating laboratory are derived views, never vendor prices.

Authorization gates remain unexecuted in this order: public-candidate technical clarification; formal quotation; material availability; ethics/rights review if applicable; contract/data-rights review; final method review including licensed standards; funding; purchase authorization; execution authorization. A fifth laboratory and its full bridge burden must be costed if a whole-laboratory holdout is later selected.

The core may be commissioned without espresso. The preferred extension has all 30 open units ×3 shots (90) and six sealed units ×3 (18). The lower-cost alternative is 18 units in three complete open groups ×3 (54). Module choice requires separate owner authority.
