# Method-provenance audit

ISO 20481 is retained only for caffeine HPLC in its stated coffee matrices. It does not support trigonelline, simultaneous pairing, sequential completeness, the bridge, or cycle/threshold values. The R0 AOAC link is removed: it resolved to an unrelated reference-table appendix and no exact applicable method identity was verified.

Casal, Oliveira, and Ferreira (1998), DOI `10.1080/10826079808001267`, directly reports simultaneous HPLC-DAD caffeine/trigonelline measurement and recovery for green and roasted coffee. da Silva et al. (2025), DOI `10.1021/acsomega.5c05526`, provides a newer validated HPLC-UV-DAD method for green and roasted Arabica extracts. These support paired quantitative analysis and recovery controls, not sequential-extraction completeness or the R0 stopping numbers.

Four exact candidate organizations remain, all `NOT_CONTACTED`. Only QSI's public service page explicitly names both caffeine and trigonelline; all candidates require technical clarification for the frozen paired SOP, preparation, moisture, and blinded aliquots.
