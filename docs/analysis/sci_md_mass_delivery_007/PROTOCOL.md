C2 stored m1/m2 means. Numerical rule: Decimal(str(recorded grams))/1000,

## Decisions frozen before outcomes

For each shot and arm e_i=predicted-observed TDS in percentage points,
R_j=sqrt(sum(d_i*e_i^2)/sum(d_i)), B_j=sum(d_i*e_i)/sum(d_i).
Report equal-shot mean R, mean abs(B), signed mean B; keep shot details private.
Zero widths are excluded from concentration denominators. Never mass-weight
across shots, invent condition groups or cancel bias before absolute values.

Coverage PASS requires N>=10 eligible physical shots AND every declared suffix
window supported and numerically qualified for all three arms, with available
positive-mass outcome chemistry. Nominal 13-shot coverage is separate. A coverage
limitation alone is not model inadequacy or positive portability.

A for each arm requires mean R<=1.00 pp, mean abs(B)<=0.50 pp AND at least
ceil(.75*N) shots satisfying BOTH individual thresholds. I1=C2 versus C1,
I0=C2 versus C0, each requires simultaneously mean R gain>=.10 pp, relative gain
>=15%, ceil(.75*N) definite paired R wins, and mean abs(B) deterioration<=.10 pp.
Both I0/I1 must PASS to earn second-summary architecture complexity. These are
new-task working budgets, not measurement errors or universal quality standards.

Use each parent's independent quadrature/refinement and <=1e-9 kg per-interval
solute allowance ceiling, including coordinate conversion. Convert to TDS via
100*allowance/d_i; bound R by weighted Euclidean norm of allowances and B by
weighted absolute mean. Bounds for absolute metrics are nonnegative. Across
shots average bounds equally; paired differences sum allowances. Relative
bounds use candidate-high/reference-low and candidate-low/reference-high;
a reference lower bound<=1e-12 pp cannot manufacture a gain. Wins require
candidate upper R < reference lower R. Count possible wins for unresolved
thresholds. Any threshold straddle is NUMERICALLY_UNRESOLVED. Never inflate
allowances. They are numerical bounds, not confidence intervals.

Fixed-denominator nonnegative lower bounds may prove failures despite missing
windows: partial squared residual sums bound R using the full declared mass;
only complete shots bound full-shot absolute bias. Missing signed residuals may
cancel, so partial bias is never a complete-panel bias failure. Unknown full mass
leaves that shot R lower bound zero. Full metrics are absent on incomplete
panels; supported-subset diagnostics stay separate. Numerical failure never
removes a slot and prevents positive decisions.

Return separate coverage, numerical, C0/C1/C2 A, I0/I1 axes. With coverage and
numerics qualified: C2 A PASS and both increments PASS gives
FROZEN_C2_TRANSFER_EARNED_ON_QUALIFIED_POOLED_PREFIX_COHORT. An adequate simpler
arm with the increment against it FAIL gives
SIMPLER_FROZEN_TRANSFER_SUFFICIENT_ON_QUALIFIED_COHORT; explicitly report C2 A.
