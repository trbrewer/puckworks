## Pressure arithmetic and shared-length consistency

Eqs.6.2–6.5 assume equal permeability/common fluid, common flow and full beds of
equal volume. They give H0=3^(4/3). p.238's 10.37 and 4.06 are derived by
multiplying permeability ratios, not independently measured pressure ratios.
Recovered figure means reproduce those printed two-decimal numbers. p.235's
approximately 3 is a looser description than the graphic's 2.398; it is not an
observation replacement. The nominal registered A/B predictions differ from 10.6.
epsilon0=0.23 yields 10.58529 as an arithmetic reconstruction, but the registered
0.236143 conventionally rounds to 0.24, not 0.23. No author intermediate is known.

Because p.219 says consolidated length enters kappa, combining kappa with full
chamber length (p.237) leaves an unresolved length/aggregation mismatch for fine.
The present declared state uses L=L0(1-lambda) on both sides, adding 0.87 to H0.
This is not an automatic finding of source error: actual bed-level estimators,
means and author intermediate inputs are unavailable.

If a supported Darcy Q/P slope is S_QP, then
kappa=mu*(L/A)*S_QP and kappa*A/(mu*L)=S_QP. Consistently reinterpreting L
while holding that slope fixed must cancel from conductance. Holding published
kappa fixed while changing lambda is instead conditional scenario sensitivity.
Neither identity permits reconstruction of mean conductance from unrelated
condition means of kappa and length. No raw slopes or covariance are invented.
