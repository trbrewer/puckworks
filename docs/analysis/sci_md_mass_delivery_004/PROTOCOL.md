## Metrics and independent decision axes

Errors in TDS percentage points. For each physical shot, with eligible suffix
masses m_i and errors e_i: R_s=sqrt(sum(m_i*e_i^2)/sum(m_i));
B_s=sum(m_i*e_i)/sum(m_i). Within condition average shot R, abs(B), and signed B.
Balance conditions equally; never pool fractions as independent replicates.
Never compare with historical six-fraction scores. Coverage accompanies every
aggregate. No outcome-dependent denominator reduction can yield full-cohort PASS.

A: in EVERY ten conditions mean R<=1.00 pp and mean abs B<=0.50 pp, with
complete declared eligible support and numerical qualification; intended missing
or source-invalid rows remain explicit and cannot earn all-cohort adequacy.
These are working research budgets, not measurement precision/universal standards.
B: versus UNANCHORED_EMPIRICAL, balanced R reduction >=20% AND >=0.10 pp,
definitely lower condition R in >=8/10, balanced mean abs B deterioration<=0.10 pp.
C: same material tests against ANCHOR_PERSISTENCE.
D: require A and versus ANCHORED_MASS candidate balanced R<=comparator+0.10 pp
and candidate balanced mean abs B<=comparator+0.10 pp.
E: separately apply B material tests against ANCHORED_MASS.
Threshold intervals crossing a boundary remain NUMERICALLY_UNRESOLVED. Definite
FAIL survives a different unsupported/unresolved gate; retain individual reasons.
Comparisons needing missing support stay not adjudicated, never changed denominators.
Adequacy, early-assay value, shape value, competitiveness and material superiority
are distinct. Report adequate comparators explicitly; no automatic preference.
A failure establishes inadequacy only when a complete condition demonstrably
fails, otherwise identify coverage limitation and preserve subset diagnostics.
