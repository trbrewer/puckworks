# Independent-review handoff protocol

After external qualification, a new independent reviewer is to reproduce C1-R4 G1/G2/G3, confirm their rejection at the frozen C1-R5 head, inspect validation ordering and independently reconstruct roots/provenance, add an unselected-record mutation, verify producer/verifier high-level separation, repeat dual exact-head replay and the uninterrupted full declared-extra suite, reproduce the export and decision-input hashes, and independently derive the actual-pilot result.

Implementer qualification is not independent acceptance, owner adjudication, approval, merge, or EWP repin. Physical validation remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
