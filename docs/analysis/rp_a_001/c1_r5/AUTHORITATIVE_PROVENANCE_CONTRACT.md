# Authoritative provenance contract

Caller self-consistency is not authority. Expected explanations and card/registry identities are reconstructed from selected repository model cards and `puckworks/models/__init__.py`. Expected questions, requirements, observation contracts, adapter identity/hash, and gate specifications are reconstructed from committed canonical constructors and frozen protocols. Selected cases and assumptions come from committed matrices. Manifest hashes are recomputed from those files.

Contract semantic fields and provenance must equal the independently reconstructed canonical contract for its question; a changed provenance plus changed caller-controlled contract hash remains invalid. Predictions must bind to a selected explanation/card, selected case, authorized observable context, and producer source. Comparisons must bind to an authorized question, requirement and pair, compatible contract, selected case/channel, and valid predictions where evidence is retained.

Neither producer nor verifier may derive the trust root from the supplied record being checked.
