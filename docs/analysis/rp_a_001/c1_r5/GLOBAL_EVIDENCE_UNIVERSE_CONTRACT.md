# Global evidence-universe contract

Every supplied material collection is parsed and primitive-validated, indexed by its typed identity, checked for duplicates, canonicalized, reference-closed, context-checked, ownership-checked, and provenance-checked before comparator applicability or any apparatus/final state is selected.

Material collections are explanations, questions, requirements, channel comparisons, contracts, predictions, measurements, gate specifications, gate evidence, gate results, apparatus aggregate, coverage edges/matrix/minimum sets, decision evidence embodied by the decision record, result cells, residuals, inventory, component reports, assumptions, and manifest roots.

Reason categories are frozen as `DANGLING_REFERENCE`, `DUPLICATE_ID`, `WRONG_TYPE_REFERENCE`, `CROSS_CONTEXT_REFERENCE`, `UNAUTHORIZED_ROOT`, `ORPHAN_RECORD`, `EXTRANEOUS_MATERIAL_RECORD`, `AUTHORITATIVE_PROVENANCE_MISMATCH`, and `CANONICAL_IDENTITY_MISMATCH`.

Invalidity is a validation error, never `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNRESOLVED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or `NOT_EVALUATED`. Legitimate incomplete evidence remains governed by the existing C1-R4 scientific-state rules.
