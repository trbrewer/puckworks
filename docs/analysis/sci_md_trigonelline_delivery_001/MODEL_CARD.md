# Research trigonelline mass-coordinate delivery

SCI-MD-TRIGONELLINE-DELIVERY-001; RESEARCH_ONLY;
NO_GOVERNING_PHYSICS_CHANGE. Scientific disposition before execution:
NOT_ADJUDICATED. This card is committed before implementation.

TR-K0 is one training-only constant concentration. TR-D0 is the fixed accepted
D0 logistic knot architecture adapted to trigonelline, with mass-only features
and a task-local 0.25 mg/g residual scale. The complete mathematical, source,
numerical and acceptance contract is [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md).

Inputs are m1, m2 and requested measured beverage-mass coordinates, in kg.
Outputs are integrated trigonelline kg/mg and interval concentration kg/kg or
mg/g, accompanied by numerical allowance and domain/extrapolation status.
No query-time chemical measurement, recipe, time or pressure is accepted.
The model does not identify kinetics, equilibrium, inventory, same-lot status
or pressure boundaries. Intervening unassayed intervals are modeled gaps,
not measured whole-cup closure. Trigonelline is already part of TDS.
