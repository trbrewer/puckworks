Authority: Hargarten et al.(2020), DOI10.1002/jsfa.10440, published methods
pp3961–3963, Eq1/2/4, and accepted
[wet-PSD](../sci_md_hargarten_wet_psd_001/RESULT.md) and
[individual-kinetics](../sci_md_hargarten_swelling_kinetics_001/RESULT.md) contracts.
Maille locators and unknown conditions are in [SOURCE.md](SOURCE.md). Comparability
classes concern the stated comparison, not a claim that the sources agree.

Hargarten materials are medium-roast100%arabica from Marcala/Honduras (valved
barrier packaging) and light-roast100%arabica Colombia/Tanzania blend (barrier
packaging without valve). Age since roast and storage interval are UNKNOWN here.
Its grinder was EK43; prepared fractions were sieved immediately after grinding.
Those conditions apply only to Hargarten, not to Maille's unidentified LD material.
