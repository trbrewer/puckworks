## One score and shot-level metrics

Freeze the clean candidate commit/tree and live bases, protocol, source and
model identities, saved-state/cohort/coordinate hashes, all 165 policy decisions
or abstentions, all candidate statuses, flags, numerical allowances and scoring
code. Public FREEZE.json and DECISION_MANIFEST.json are byte copies of immutable
private bindings. A fresh independent reviewer must approve that exact freeze,
checking zero fits, models, source/cohort, observed endpoints, information
separation, stopping intersection, comparators, allowances, acceptance rules,
rights and clean candidate. Absent approval: READY_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW,
zero scores. Approval binds the exact freeze SHA256/head/tree/base.

The scorer first verifies approval and immutable hashes, then exclusively
creates a score-start receipt. Only then it parses the source once and joins
the frozen selected vials with outcome chemistry. It cannot regenerate or
alter decisions. A completion receipt binds all outputs. Any existing start,
result or completion refuses replay; failed exposed attempts remain. A scientific
defect after exposure requires owner adjudication, with no automatic rescore.

For each complete recorded vial use the accepted percent/100 mass-fraction
conversion and grams/1000 beverage conversion. Sum mass*concentration from
the anchor through the selected vial; suffix TDS is 100*sum_solute/sum_mass.
Use Decimal(str(source float)) with 28-digit arithmetic for these displayed
source values, avoiding threshold equality artifacts from binary accumulation.
No measurement-error interval or additional tolerance is invented.

The observational oracle is the earliest recorded endpoint satisfying both
measured requirements, or NO_OBSERVED_FEASIBLE_ENDPOINT. It is outcome-only
and exists solely for regret/missed-opportunity accounting. It is never an
input to decisions or an available prospective comparator.

Classify each shot/policy/contract as SUCCESS, FALSE_FEASIBLE, ABSTAIN_CORRECT,
ABSTAIN_MISSED_FEASIBLE or NUMERICALLY_UNRESOLVED. Source/identity/support
failures block separately. Abstentions are not successful delivered decisions.
Report eligible shots, qualified decisions, each classification count, observed
feasible shots, feature-extrapolated shots, successes/decisions and successes/N.
Privately retain predicted and measured margins at selected endpoints; false
feasible severity is max(0,S_min-S_observed)*1e6 mg and max(0,q_min-q_observed)
percentage points. Report maximum deficits for every policy and contract.

For successful selections, regret is selected cumulative mass minus the oracle
mass, in grams. Report median/max regret, exact-oracle counts and successful
choices later than oracle. For false-feasible selections with a later observed
feasible boundary, privately report the additional mass needed to reach the
first such boundary. No p-values, independent-vial claims or contract averaging.
