## What the contrasts establish

The central delayed rise remains visible in the full-profile figure and the late-band ledger. Resolved contrary orderings defeat an all-six statement on that support. One unresolved or unsupported request leaves an all-six statement unestablished. Even six positive primary contrasts establish neither monotonicity nor whole-section coverage nor a population trend. Printed radii do not supply whole-bed volume weights.

DeltaA compares two printed coordinate windows. Matching capsule identity and coordinates do not establish material registration or an unchanged observation operator. Roast, capsule and scan delay are confounded; immediate/dark and delayed/medium are separate histories, not a roast or drying-time experiment.

## Source review versus graphical/numerical qualification

The final journal article controls methods and captions. Its Figure 14 medium history is before/three days after extraction; Figure 16 dark history is before/immediately after. Final pages 12/13 and arXiv v2 pages 14/15 contain matching segment geometries within production rounding, counted once. The separate seven-page final supplement was inspected. The 1-hour and 24-hour mentions are narrative-only, not extra profiles. See [SOURCE_NOTES.md](SOURCE_NOTES.md) for identities, rights, masks and locators.

Dry segmentation matches a reference porosity (0.17); wet segmentation manually selects a threshold between histogram peaks. Wet imaging cannot adequately distinguish liquid from solid. These observation limitations, segmentation sensitivity, repeatability and physical variability are unquantified and are not covered by graphical enclosures.

Nominal means integrate the retained straight segments analytically with partial boundary segments and the affine Jacobian. Qualified bounds cover the entire declared panel-shared calibration set and horizontal/vertical local stroke allowances using interval integration. The fixed 4×4 parameter boxes and 256 cells per band cover continuous sets; endpoints and every enclosed PL vertex bound each cell. This is an outer relaxation, not an optimizer estimate or a jointly attainable reconstruction. No bootstrap, p-values or experimental confidence interval is used. [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) fixes decisions before computation; [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md) separates software checks and hosted status from [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md).

## Constructed identification witness

For the deliberately idealized g=phi(1−S), exact rational arithmetic verifies physical ranges 0≤phi,S≤1 and the same g_lower=3/25=0.12, g_upper=2/25=0.08 in all three cases:

| phi (lower,upper) | S (lower,upper) | phi contrast |
|---|---|---|
| (6/25,4/25) | (1/2,1/2) | +2/25 = +0.08 |
| (1/5,1/5) | (2/5,3/5) | 0 |
| (4/25,6/25) | (1/4,2/3) | −2/25 = −0.08 |

This is a constructed nonidentification witness, not a fit to Mo, scanner calibration or assertion that the plotted signal equals gas volume. Even that favorable idealization does not identify porosity ordering with unknown spatial saturation. Displayed signal contrast and difference of displayed contrasts are the only numerical targets. Geometric porosity ordering/change and identification of fines migration, erosion, swelling or consolidation are separate, unestablished claims.
