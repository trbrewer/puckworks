## Limits of this permission

- It is **not** an SPDX or OSI licence. Do not describe the upstream repository or its contents as MIT,
  CC-BY, public domain, or open-source licensed, and do not state or imply that Dr. Grudeva relicensed the
  upstream repository under the Puckworks MIT licence.
- Distributing the covered material inside the Puckworks package does **not** place it under the
  repository's MIT licence. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- It does **not** extend automatically to future upstream files, or to material in that repository whose
  ownership cannot reasonably be attributed to Dr. Grudeva.
- It clears **no** unrelated third-party material and **no** other component. A rights record is
  per-component and never propagates.
- Article- and thesis-derived material keeps its **separate** CC-BY provenance; article rights, code
  rights, data rights, and generated-output rights remain distinct records.
- It authorizes nothing about material Puckworks does not currently ship. The upstream `raw.xlsx` / raw
  per-shot CSV are **still not redistributed here**; adding them would be a separate scope decision.
- Attribution to Dr. Yoana Grudeva, to the upstream repository, and to the associated publication is
  preserved everywhere the material is used.

## What this permission does not change

It resolves a **legal/repository-use** block and nothing else. The component's evidence strength stays
`post_fit_reconstruction`, its provenance class stays `published_port`, RC-2 stays verification-gated
until the forthcoming companion dataset lands, and its outputs remain on a grain-volume concentration
basis that has no tested inventory-conserving conversion to the bed-volume basis the common scenario
carries. Permission is not validation, comparability, or applicability.
