# Dias and Benassi (2015)

- DOI: `10.3390/beverages1030127`
- Access: open article, CC BY 4.0; normalized factual intake only.
- Data: Table 2, paired caffeine/trigonelline means and SD for known Arabica/Robusta blends at three roast mass-loss levels, explicitly dry basis.
- Method: HPLC-DAD, external calibration; duplicate analyses; recovery and detection limits reported.
- Gate use: direct total-roasted-content rows may enter the primary cohort. All rows remain one publication/data lineage/laboratory group; blends do not satisfy pure-species minima.
