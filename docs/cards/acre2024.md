# Acre et al. (2024)

- DOI: `10.21577/0103-5053.20240031`
- Access: open JBCS/SciELO article and supplement; normalized factual intake only, no PDF committed.
- Data: 10 conilon (`C1–C10`), 10 robusta (`R1–R10`), and 10 intervarietal hybrids (`H1–H10`); paired caffeine/trigonelline means and SD in Tables 1–2; genotype metadata in Table S1.
- Basis and roast: explicitly dry basis; roasted moisture `2.4 ± 0.2 g/100 g`; roast mass loss `13.7 ± 1.7 g/100 g`; medium-light roast, about 8 min at `210 ± 10 °C`; `L*=32 ± 3`, hue `26 ± 4`.
- Method: HPLC-DAD, six-point external calibration in triplicate; duplicate extraction and analysis (`n=4`); LOD/LOQ reported.
- Gate use: direct total-roasted-content rows may enter the primary cohort. Conilon, robusta and hybrids are varieties of *C. canephora*, not separate species. All rows share one publication/laboratory group.
- Lineage caveat: shared authors, institution, methods and some cultivar names with Viencz 2023 are recorded. Source-described locations/campaigns differ; reuse of the same experimental lot was not established.
