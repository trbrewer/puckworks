# Viencz et al. (2023)

- DOI: `10.1016/j.jfca.2023.105140`
- Access: author-deposited full article in the Embrapa Alice public repository; normalized factual intake, no PDF committed.
- Data: Tables 1–2 and supplementary Tables S2–S3, 57 Robusta clones and 10 named intervarietal hybrids, paired caffeine/trigonelline means and SD, dry basis.
- Method: HPLC-DAD; duplicate extraction and analysis (`n=4`); same-batch roasted moisture and Agtron range reported.
- Gate use: direct total-roasted-content rows may enter the primary cohort. All are *C. canephora* and share one UEL/Embrapa analytical lineage and validation group.
- Lineage caveat: seven BRS cultivar names also occur in Acre 2024. The reported campaigns/locations differ, so no same-lot identity is asserted; the shared laboratory remains explicit.
