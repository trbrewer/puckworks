# Model card: Zhai 2022 — ultrasound-assisted cold brew (UAC) characterization

**Paper/thesis:** X. Zhai, M. Yang, J. Zhang, L. Zhang, Y. Tian, C. Li, L. Bao, C. Ma, A. M. Abd El-Aty, "Feasibility of Ultrasound-Assisted Extraction for Accelerated Cold Brew Coffee Processing: Characterization and Comparison With Conventional Brewing Methods," *Frontiers in Nutrition* **9**:849811 (2022). DOI 10.3389/fnut.2022.849811. CC BY 4.0.
**Stage(s):** observables (nominal only) · **Kind:** calibration at best (offline dataset; no physics executed)
**Status:** proposed (card-only)

## Scope and mechanism
Experimental method-comparison paper. Five preparations of one Brazilian arabica at a nominally fixed coffee:water ratio — hot boiled (HB, 95 °C, 5 min), pour-over (PO, 92 °C, ~3 min, three-stage), static cold immersion at 4 °C and 10 °C for 12 h (4CB, 10CB), and a new **ultrasound-assisted cold brew** (UAC: probe sonication, 200 W nominal, "room temperature," 60 min) — are characterized for EY, TDS, pH, CIE L\*a\*b\*, lumped extraction rates of phenolics/lipids/protein/titratable acid, HPLC caffeine/CGA/trigonelline, and 97 HS-SPME-GC/MS volatiles with odor activity values. A three-factor, three-level orthogonal array (L9(3³)) over ratio × time × ultrasonic power is then used to pick a UAC operating point.

No transport, kinetic, or constitutive model is proposed. There is no bed, no pressure, no flow field, no permeability, and — critically — **no time axis**: every method contributes a single endpoint. The stated mechanism for the ultrasound effect (acoustic cavitation, sonoporation, "ultrasonic capillary effect") is narrative, cited to review literature, and never quantified; the authors say the capillary mechanism "is not fully understood."

## Governing equations
Three definitional relations from Methods; nothing physical is printed and nothing is simplified away because nothing exists to simplify.

1. **Extraction yield (§Physicochemical Analysis):** EY (%) = (W₂ − W₁)/W₀ × 100 %
   - W₂ — mass of evaporating dish plus dried extract [g]; W₁ — empty dish mass [g]; W₀ — initial dry coffee mass used in the extraction [g]. Gravimetric solids-per-dose; the aliquot-to-batch scaling is not stated.
2. **Total phenolic extraction rate (§TPC):** TPC (%) = c · V · N / W × 100 %
   - c — Folin–Ciocalteu phenolic concentration as gallic-acid equivalents [µg mL⁻¹]; V — extract volume [mL]; N — dilution factor [–]; W — "coffee mass corresponding to the coffee extraction" [µg].
   - *Unit flag: as printed the numerator is µg and W is in µg, so the ratio is dimensionless and the extra ×100 % is consistent only if the result is a percentage of dose. The same ×100-with-percent-output ambiguity was recorded for* `cordoba2019` *Eq. 2; treat the tabulated numbers, not the printed constants, as authoritative.*
3. **Odor activity value (§Volatile Compound Analysis):** OAVᵢ = Cᵢ / OTᵢ
   - Cᵢ — concentration of aroma compound *i* in the extract [µg L⁻¹]; OTᵢ — literature odor threshold in water [µg L⁻¹] (their refs 15, 31, 37). Compounds with OAV > 1 are held to contribute.

TL, Tpro and TA have **no equations at all** — only protocols (Soxhlet/petroleum ether gravimetry; Kjeldahl; titration of 20 mL to pH 6.5 with 0.1 mol/L NaOH, reported as CGA equivalents). Each is described as "amount extracted from coffee per unit mass," a denominator the paper never defines numerically.

## Calibration and validation offered by the source
No model is validated. As a dataset the internal quality is mixed, and four defects are decisive.

**(a) The UAC thermal history is unreported and almost certainly not "cold."** 200 W nominal for 3600 s into a ~330 g charge is 720 kJ. Even at 5 % electro-acoustic coupling that is 36 kJ, an adiabatic rise of **26 K**; at a more typical 20 % it is 104 K, i.e. boiling. Holding the charge within 5 K of ambient with no cooling requires dissipating **< 2 W, under 1 % of nominal**. The paper reports no jacket, no bath, no duty cycle, and no temperature measurement during sonication. Either the delivered power was far below nominal, or cooling was applied and not described, or the "cold brew" ran hot. Nothing in the paper distinguishes these. The headline claim — that ultrasound substitutes for 12 h of cold contact time — is therefore confounded with an uncontrolled and unmeasured temperature rise, which is the single most obvious alternative explanation for every enhancement reported. The authors do not raise it.

**(b) Table 4 (OAVs) has a corrupted compound-name column for rows 21–29.** Rows 21–29 repeat verbatim the names of rows 1–9 while carrying different thresholds, different OAVs, and different odor descriptors. Back-computing Cᵢ = OAVᵢ · OTᵢ and matching against Table 3 identifies all nine uniquely and exactly across all five methods (agreement to the printed 2 d.p. in every non-empty cell):

| 22 | 2-N-butyl furan | **Guaiacol** (#66) | 2.54 / 4.92 / 1.94 / 1.89 / 1.77 |
| 23 | 5-Methylfuran aldehyde | 4-Ethyl-2-methoxyphenol (#67) | 3.71 / 3.00 / – / 2.66 / – |

The reconstruction is confirmed by the paper's own prose: the text attributes the dominant caramel note to **guaiacol (OAV > 3000)**, a compound that appears nowhere in Table 4 as printed — it is row 22. The corrected assignment also recovers the class census the text states (phenols 5, pyridines 2, ester 1). Rows 1–20 check out against their printed names and need no correction. Residual discrepancy: the corrected census gives 7 pyrazines and 4 furans against the text's 6 and 5; not resolvable from the printed material.

**(c) The Conclusions contradict Table 2 and the Results text.** The abstract/Conclusions state UAC gave "comparable contents of caffeine, CGA, and trigonelline." Table 2 places UAC **lowest of all five methods** on caffeine (0.56, significance letter d) and CGA (1.25), i.e. −6.7 to −17.6 % on caffeine and −3.1 to −18.3 % on CGA against every comparator, and the Results section says plainly that UAC caffeine and CGA "were significantly lower than that of static cold brews." Take the Results text and Table 2; the Conclusions overstate.

**(d) A species-specific outlier the authors do not address.** Between 4CB and 10CB — same 12 h, 6 K apart — caffeine is identical (0.60 / 0.60) and CGA nearly so (1.37 / 1.35), but trigonelline drops 38 % (0.50 → 0.31), a separate significance letter from every other cell in the column. Trigonelline is highly water-soluble and thermally stable at these temperatures; a 38 % species-specific swing with no movement in the co-extracted species is more consistent with an analytical artifact than with a 6 K solubility effect. Unremarked.

**What does hold up.** Table 3 is internally consistent: summing the UAC column by class reproduces the text's stated values exactly (furans 19.73 µg mL⁻¹ = 40.30 %, pyrazines 9.64 = 19.69 %, pyridines 4.11, pyrroles 4.04, ketones 3.39, phenols 3.05, total 48.96). Table 3 is the one block of this paper whose arithmetic is demonstrably clean. It remains semi-quantitative — 97 analytes against a single internal standard (4-methyl-2-amyl alcohol) with library-only identification (NIST14, no retention indices, no authentic standards), so absolute concentrations carry compound-dependent response-factor error of unknown size.

**Statistics.** n = 3, one-way ANOVA + Duncan at 5 %. The design is not analyzed as the factorial it is (method is treated as a single one-way factor); the L9 orthogonal array is analyzed by K/R range statistics only, with no ANOVA, no error term, and no confirmation run reported beyond a single optimized batch.

## Assumptions and validity range
- **Regime is unpressurized batch immersion, not espresso.** Atmospheric pressure, 1–12 h, no tamped puck, no percolation, no flow. PO is the only percolating method and it is characterized by endpoint only. None of `packing`, `machine`, `infiltration`, `flow`, or `bed_dynamics` is exercised.
- **No time axis.** Single endpoint per method; extraction kinetics are discussed only by citing Fuller & Rao. Nothing here constrains a rate constant.
- **Grind is one sieve pass** (< ~841 µm upper cut). No PSD, no fines fraction, no mean, no lower bound, no distribution shape. `GrindState` cannot be populated even approximately.
- **Roast degree is never reported**, which forecloses cross-comparison with any registered chemistry dataset.
- **Stated ratio is internally inconsistent:** "the same coffee to water ratio (1:18)" against 16.5 g / 313.5 g = 1:19.0. A 1:18 ratio implies 297 g water. Small, but it means the nominal ratio and the nominal masses cannot both be right, and the OED varies ratio as a factor.
- **The UAC temperature is unmeasured** (above). Every UAC-vs-CB difference is confounded by it.
- **Component extraction rates do not close.** Reading Fig. 1A for UAC gives TPC + TL + Tpro + TA ≈ 10.5 points of dose against the only main-text EY (17.72 %) — 59 % of extracted solids, with lipids alone at 27 %. A paper-filtered brew whose extracted solids are a quarter lipid is not credible (`gloess2013` measures esterified fatty acids below 0.2 w-% of an *unfiltered espresso* beverage; the Fig. 1A TL converts to ≈0.25 w-% of beverage here, i.e. a filtered cold brew matching or exceeding espresso in lipid). Either the four rates are on different, undeclared denominators, or the Soxhlet gravimetry is capturing more than lipid. The paper defines none of the denominators, so this cannot be resolved from the article.
- Chemistry is three resolved species (caffeine, CGA, trigonelline) plus four lumped aggregates. No sugars, no individual acids, no melanoidins.
- Silent on: pressure, permeability, porosity, bed geometry, particle-scale transport, mass conservation, any transient, temperature as a continuous variable, and the mechanism it names (cavitation is never characterized — no calorimetry, no cavitation yield, no probe geometry or tip immersion depth).
- **Failure mode if repurposed:** using any of these numbers as espresso targets is a regime error; using Table 4 as printed propagates nine mislabelled compounds; using the abstract's alkaloid claim contradicts the paper's own Table 2.

## Interface mapping
Inputs consumed: none. No `GrindState` field can be set (single sieve cut, no PSD); no `BedState` field exists (no bed); `MachineState` is inapplicable (atmospheric, no pump, no pressure profile) — the ultrasonic probe is not representable in contracts v0.1 and would need a stage that does not exist.
Outputs produced: nominally `ShotResultState.EY_pct` and `tds_pct`, but Eq. 1 is the same gravimetric definition the pipeline already implements, and the five-method EY/TDS values live in a supplementary table absent from the article PDF. TPC/TL/Tpro/TA, volatiles, OAVs, and L\*a\*b\* have no contract field.
Couplings: **none.** Not runtime; not an offline calibration provider for any registered component. No adapters warranted — there is nothing on either side to adapt.

## Extractable data
- **Table 3** → 97 volatiles × 5 methods in absolute µg mL⁻¹. The only clean, complete, internally verified block, and unusually absolute for this genre (`cordoba2019` reports relative areas only). Still: cold/hot immersion regime, no time axis, single-IS semi-quantitation, unreported roast. Not worth a file under espresso scope.
- **Table 2** → caffeine/CGA/trigonelline × 5 methods, n = 3. Small, and the 4CB/10CB trigonelline outlier is unexplained.
- **Table 4** → OAVs, 29 rows. **Do not transcribe as printed.** If ever wanted, transcribe with the row-21–29 corrections tabulated above and a provenance note that the correction is `[RS inference]` back-computed from Cᵢ = OAVᵢ · OTᵢ against Table 3, not an author erratum.
- **Fig. 1A** → TPC/TL/Tpro/TA bar chart, figure-read only, denominators undefined, fails the closure check above.
- **Supplementary Tables 1–4** (EY/TDS/pH/color for the five methods; HPLC regression equations; OED L9 table; optimized-condition characterization) and **Supplementary Figures 1–2** (TICs; single-factor sweeps) are open-access at the Frontiers article URL and are where the actual EY/TDS numbers live. Acquisition is cheap but **not recommended** — the numbers land on no registry slot.
- Raw data / code: none published. Statement is "original contributions are included in the article/Supplementary Material, further inquiries to the corresponding author." No repository, no chromatograms, no traces.

## Overlaps and conflicts
- **`cordoba2019` (skip, cold-brew factorial) — near-duplicate genre, mutually non-superseding.** Same regime (cold immersion), same aggregate chemistry (TPC/TA/pH), same GC-MS endpoint profiling, same absence of a model. Zhai adds absolute volatile concentrations, HPLC alkaloids, and the ultrasound arm; Cordoba adds a real grind factor (two sized bins) and a sensory panel. Neither advances the registry. This card's regime-skip reasoning is the same as Cordoba's and should be read alongside it.
- **`angeloni2023` (data-only) — dominates on the multi-class-solute backlog.** 66 espresso shots × 8 resolved species across a 3T × 3p × 3grind design, in regime. Zhai offers 3 resolved species at 5 endpoints out of regime. No contest.
- **`schmieder2023` (calibration) — dominates on kinetics.** Fraction-resolved concentration-vs-cumulative-mass curves with fitted (c₀, λ) per species on a DE1. Zhai has no time axis at all.
- **`gloess2013` (data-only) — direct conflict on lipids.** Gloess measures esterified fatty acids < 0.2 w-% of an unfiltered espresso beverage; Zhai's Fig. 1A TL implies ≈0.25 w-% in a paper-filtered cold brew. Flagged above as a closure failure on Zhai's side.
- **`liang2021` (calibration, immersion equilibrium desorption) — the model Zhai lacks.** Liang supplies exactly the endpoint-only immersion physics (K·E_max, brew-ratio scaling) that Zhai's five endpoints would need to be interpreted. Zhai reports no brew-ratio sweep at fixed method and no retention correction, so it cannot even test Liang's ratio-independence prediction.
- **Backlog "observables: temperature effects" — brushed and missed.** Five preparations span 4 → 95 °C, which looks like a temperature sweep, but temperature is fully confounded with time (12 h vs 5 min), method, and — for UAC — an unmeasured sonication rise. It constrains no thermal kernel.
- **Backlog "extraction: multi-class solute chemistry" — not advanced.** Three species, no time axis, no flow.
- **`ellero2019_jfe`** tracks the same three species (caffeine, trigonelline, CGA) *with* a transient; Zhai's endpoints are not comparable to it (different regime, no conditions overlap).
- Competes with nothing registered; complements nothing; supersedes nothing. No contact with `cameron2020.extraction_bdf`, `brewer2026.*`, `wadsworth2026.*`, or `foster2025.infiltration`.
- **Genre note, consistent with the `wale2023` heuristic:** the discriminating question is a time axis and a flow field. This paper has an explicit brewing-method comparison and a mass-transfer narrative, so it clears the "is it really about extraction" filter that `legasmuhammed2021`/`yu2021`/`mystkowska2024`/`wale2023` failed — and still fails on both discriminators. Worth recording that regime (unpressurized batch immersion) is now the operative skip criterion for this cluster, not analyte relevance.
