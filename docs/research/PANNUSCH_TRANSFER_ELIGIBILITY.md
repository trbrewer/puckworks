# Pannusch transfer eligibility

The March 2022 campaign is source-designated prediction data separated from the source
fit, but its targets are public and exposed. It can support a later fixed-parameter,
source-internal comparison; it cannot support target-blind or independent-validation
claims.

The source provides eight conditions × three physical shots × six assayed fractions,
with caffeine, trigonelline, 5-CQA, workbook CQA sum, and TDS concentrations and derived
fraction masses. Inputs are programmed, not measured puck-face conditions. Conditions
C01, C02, C05, and C06 are constant-temperature/constant-flow; C07 and C08 are prescribed
flow ramps. These six are conditionally eligible for normalized fraction-shape EWP
comparison after an exact consumer adapter and frozen fair baselines. C03 and C04 are
blocked because current EWP production chemistry lacks qualified time-varying temperature
physics. All absolute-mass comparisons remain blocked by the unestablished inventory
bridge.

Prescribed-flow replay cannot validate pressure, permeability, wetting, retention,
puck filling, first drip, or hydraulics. Separate caffeine and trigonelline runs are not
coupled multispecies simulation. The source exponential fits are target-fitted descriptive
references; source RSM privileges require a comparison-specific audit. Fair candidates
are calibration-only pooled mean, nearest condition, interpolation, condition-invariant
endpoint, and no-ramp-effect baselines.

The next task is therefore a narrow `XSV-PANNUSCH-TRANSFER-001`, limited initially to
normalized fraction shapes in C01, C02, C05–C08. Its maximum claim is
`CAMPAIGN_SEPARATED_TARGET_EXPOSED_FIXED_PARAMETER_COMPARISON`. No scoring is performed
here.
