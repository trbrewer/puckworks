# Joint-clock execution and interpretation

The owner approved the plan and added λ=0.05 before any fit. λ=1 is primary; 0.05, 0.5 and 2 are predeclared sensitivities. No weight is selected by held-out results. This is a retrospective capability comparison on one previously exposed cohort. The fitted scale lag has an unidentified physical mechanism and remains in the observation layer.

## Evidence and clocks

The authenticated FORM_A source inputs contain 56 distinct `waszkiewicz2025/traces_per_brew` histories, with the original alias exclusion and eleven nominal conditions. Original files, configuration hashes and saved D0 tuples are checked by the unchanged wetting-screen loader. The retained native A1 concentration-bound qualification flags remain visible; they do not remove brews. Fixed viscosity makes chemistry passive with respect to liquid flow.

All models use raw line pressure clipped at zero, with the inherited line-to-inlet convention. Through 100 s, pressure interpolation uses FORM_A's retained 1,000-point input history, preserving comparability with the screen. Original clean-course pressure samples beyond 100 s extend it to cover D0's longer fitting window; pressure is never shifted by breakthrough or scale lag. This is distinct from the original D0 replication, which replays D0's saved forcing grids and evaluators exactly.

The fixed R0 chemistry is the screen's 256-cell release/storage/transport column: release rate 0.15/s, capacity 180 kg/m³, diffusivity 10⁻⁹ m²/s, unchanged initial inventory, geometry, porosity and viscosity. Its engineering assumptions are retained. There is no chemistry fitting, observed-mass/TDS predictor input, or concentration-to-viscosity feedback. The column includes filling storage, explicit capacity-limited release, implicit upwind transport, diffusion and inlet diffusive loss. Dissolved mass and remaining extractable solid carry through breakthrough. Native verification is inherited by exact predictor hash; this does not establish continuum or physical validation.

J0 starts fully wet at stable-pressure zero, with fresh inventory and no dissolved solute. J1 uses the chosen phenomenological W1 trajectory x_f/L=√(t/τ0), capped at one. J2 uses the pressure integral in PLAN.md. Before breakthrough, outlet liquid mass M and flow are zero. Afterwards M starts at zero and evolves with form A. The exact breakthrough event is inserted into the numerical time grid. As in the verified screen, the step completing saturation has no outflow; subsequent saturated steps use the second-order hydraulic update.

J2's scale sees beverage mass B_bed(t−t_s) and liquid flow q_bed(t−t_s), both zero before the delayed trace. Pressure, front motion, dissolution and recovery keep their absolute bed times. An inlet delay or delayed bed outflow would be a different model. Unshifted J2 bed-flow scores are diagnostics; scored flow uses the shifted scale signal. Liquid ρQ is scored against scale-derived beverage flow, preserving the inherited convention; endpoint beverage-minus-liquid flow remains an ungraded diagnostic.

## Fold isolation and fitting

There are 144 joint fits: three model families, four weights and twelve training sets. The eleven ordinary leave-one-condition-out folds exclude every brew at the held condition. A single fitted vector and the matching training-derived fixed Mc apply to every training and held-out brew in that fold. DESCRIPTIVE_ALL is separate and supplies constant-pressure curves. No omission twins, Mc refits, pressure-conditioned parameter vectors or refit-bootstrap campaign are added.

The composite fitting criterion averages within-brew duration-normalized flow MSE and onset loss, first within each pressure condition and then across conditions. Its fixed normalizations are 0.437 g/s and 4.534 s; these are not noise estimates. Flow and onset share measured histories and are not independent likelihoods. Fitting flow masks are D0's observed-onset+2-s through clean-end samples on the 0.5-s grid. They never depend on predicted onset. The state integrates through all masked intervals without resetting. The detector uses its separate fixed 0–100-s contract.

Optimization uses bounded Powell in log(a), log(Pstar), n, log(Pd), and, for J2, log(k_w0), log(Pw), t_s/100; J1 uses τ0/100. Domains remain those in PLAN.md, including n≥1 without an upper fitting bound. Each fold uses the matching historical D0 flow fit with its matching W1 timing start (J1), or W2/W2T starts (J2). Zero-delay/zero-lag boundary starts are retained. Sixteen Latin-hypercube starts, seed 20261103, cover the declared finite transformed domains with n initially in [1,8]. There are 17/18/19 starts per J0/J1/J2 fit: 2,592 scheduled starts overall. No cross-fold or full-cohort seeds enter an ordinary fold.

Powell uses xtol=10⁻⁴ and ftol=10⁻⁷ with no iteration, evaluation or wall-clock cap. The lowest finite sampled training objective is retained, including starting points; all optimizer outcomes, competing solutions and distances to domain bounds are recorded. Exact sampled ties and their parameter ranges describe sampled non-identification, not confidence intervals or proof of a unique/global minimum. Similar objectives with different flow/delay vectors remain visible in OPTIMIZER_STARTS.csv. Training sets overlap heavily, so fold stability does not identify a mechanism.

An execution repair enforces these same bounds during continuation: Powell extrapolated J1's delay to −2.7756×10⁻¹⁵ s at the zero boundary, making its square-root trajectory nonfinite. The failed attempt is retained. The continuation helper projects optimizer coordinates onto the declared domain, reuses exact recorded trial values, retains completed starts and resumes the interrupted start and remaining frozen schedule. It changes no domain, model, objective, start or tolerance. Failed-attempt and continuation CPU time are separate; no unavailable prediction becomes a censored onset. The original frozen fitting source remains unchanged.

Search uses 0.02 s. All reported capability scores come from frozen parameters re-evaluated at 0.005 s. The 0.02-s scores, their differences, detector changes and extrema evidence remain available. FITS.csv and TRAINING_LOSS_COMPONENTS.csv explicitly label 0.02-s optimizer diagnostics; they are not substituted for final held-out scores. LOSS_BY_PRESSURE.csv gives held-out components at both steps, including unweighted and λ-weighted onset contributions. A non-detection remains a lower-bound loss even during optimization.

## Exact prefix evaluation during search

Hydraulics always spans the full fixed flow support. Chemistry uses the unchanged column. During fitting, prefixes at bed times 20, 50 and 100 s may avoid computing chemistry after the first confirmed onset. The observer is applied only where the original sample sequence is available; the last sixteen samples of an incomplete prefix are excluded because differentiation needs one future sample and the centered 31-sample filter needs fifteen more. Consequently an early confirmed onset has its complete differentiation, filter and two-second interval support, identical to the full calculation. If no onset has been confirmed, the column is extended through the entire required observation support. Censor bounds and sample counts always come from that original full contract.

This is exact prefix evaluation, not a chemistry lookup or surrogate. Structural tests compare it with full traces, including low-flow non-detections, long lags, wetting-state carryover and the unshifted hydraulic update. The separate PREFIX_VERIFICATION record compares the full training objectives and pressure components on all 56 histories at the three matching descriptive historical starts. Final 0.005/0.02-s predictions store complete column traces through clean end; they do not use prefix termination.

## Observation, censoring and scoring

B=B_liquid+B_solute,out is sampled onto the original observation timestamps before `numpy.gradient` and B0c's 31-sample, order-one Savitzky–Golay filter. The model cup baseline over stable-pressure −2 to 0 s is zero. Strict mass>0.5 g and flow>0.1 g/s must persist for at least 2 s; onset is the interval start. No outflow is not a zero-TDS measurement.

A valid non-detection contributes max(0,c_i−observed onset)², where c_i is the latest original sample with T_last−t_j≥2 s. NaN from sustained_onset is handled as a valid non-detection. Missing/corrupt evidence is unavailable, not censored, and never changes the horizon or silently reduces the cohort. Terminal mass is not extrapolated. Front-arrival censoring at 100 s is separate. Medians include every intended brew; if censoring prevents identification, a median bound is reported rather than a detected-only median.

Final endpoint, primary and companion flow scores use FORM_A's exact source masks and the inherited mean of condition RMSEs. This reporting aggregation differs from the mean squared loss used for fitting. D0's original flow-only replay uses its original support, saved fits and Mc bank: it is not the common-mask table. The common-mask D0 benchmark reuses the authenticated screen Z predictions, whose liquid clock is D0's original M(0)=0 clock; its added fresh-bed R0 beverage observer is explicitly diagnostic and does not revise D0's historical onset disposition. B1 has hydraulic scores only. Retained W0/W1/W2/W2T results are re-scored without new fits.

Existing onset bands remain MEETS≤3.51 s, NEAR≤4.53 s, otherwise MISSES. With censoring, a bound exceeding 4.53 s is MISSES; otherwise it is NOT_SCORED/unresolved. Flow accuracy retains the inherited paired comparison and ±0.25-g/s equivalence band. Nominal peak and ≥9-bar slope grades reuse the existing helpers; the slope is the regression over 9/11/13-bar condition means. Individual 1/2/3.5-bar hydraulic residuals remain visible. No overall pass/fail criterion or new capability gate is introduced.

J1−J0, J2−J0 and J2−J1 comparisons at each weight, plus separate D0 contrasts, use identical 1,000 condition-then-whole-brew draws, seed 20261103. Both scores and the paired difference are reported. All intervals are conditional on fitted parameters and fixed Mc. Intervals on a censored score describe its computed bound; paired bound differences are not bounds on unknown true-RMSE differences. The supplementary RMS of nine condition-median signed errors at 3.5–13 bar suppresses within-condition variation and does not replace the 56-brew score or per-brew evidence.

## Extrema, artifacts and scope

Each model/weight has one DESCRIPTIVE_ALL constant-pressure curve on the inherited 23-pressure grid, a 100-s horizon and 0.1-s detector timestamps. Physical-front minima, detector minima and finite-time flow maxima are separate outputs. Grid ties, edge minima and censor-obscured minima are explicit. Flow interpolation is a local three-point numerical diagnostic, not a statistical interval. Held-out nominal extrema mix ordinary folds and are reported separately. Pw is the front minimum of J2's constant-pressure wetting law, not the detector minimum. A flat J0/J1 front curve has no unique pressure optimum.

Source hashes, the pre-fit freeze, every completed/failed attempt, selected fits, complete predictions and process CPU hours are retained. Large native traces, trial histories and prediction arrays stay outside Git. Summary/CSV/figure artifacts live here; no earlier results or EWP files change. Conclusions are confined to the tested coupled models and this retrospective cohort. A scale lag has no identified physical mechanism, and no successor work follows automatically.
