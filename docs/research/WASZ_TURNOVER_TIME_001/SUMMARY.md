# Waszkiewicz pressure–flow snapshots

**Yes: the 9/11/13-bar decline appears by stable-pressure +30 s and is already
present at detected-onset +10 s; it strengthens and then weakens, rather than
emerging only at 100 s.** All **56 brews × 10 windows = 560** observations have
recorded support, across all eleven conditions. No low-pressure window is missing;
65 stable-clock windows start before detected onset and remain included.

The unweighted slope across three condition means, using matched achieved line
pressure, is **+0.0027 [−0.0037, +0.0101]** at stable +10 s, **−0.0296
[−0.0599, +0.0040]** at +20 s, **−0.0934 [−0.1614, −0.0209]** at +30 s,
**−0.1442 [−0.2144, −0.0722]** at +40 s, **−0.0768 [−0.1014, −0.0512]**
at +60 s, and **−0.0638 [−0.0852, −0.0417] g/s/bar** at +100 s. The paired
100-minus-10 change is **−0.0665 [−0.0861, −0.0462]**; 100-minus-40 is
**+0.0804 [+0.0077, +0.1406]**. The observed maximum among condition means
shifts from nominal **3.5 bar** at 10/20 s to **6 bar** at 30–100 s.

On the onset clock, slopes at 10/20/30/40 s are **−0.0441 / −0.1220 /
−0.1191 / −0.0729 g/s/bar**, all with pointwise intervals below zero. Paired
20-minus-10 is **−0.0778 [−0.1071, −0.0506]** and 40-minus-30 is **+0.0462
[+0.0102, +0.0866]**. The observed maximum stays at **6 bar**. There are no
point-estimate ties or edge maxima. The 13-bar/maximum ratio rises from
**0.088 to 0.784** on the stable clock and **0.427 to 0.785** on the onset clock.

The strengthening decline comes from unequal increases: stable +10→40 s flow
at nominal 9/11/13 bar rises **0.008→1.739 / 0.021→1.487 / 0.018→1.201 g/s**.
13-bar flow subsequently catches up. The separate exact Stage A endpoint
reproduces **−0.0655064 g/s/bar** and its **6-bar** maximum; its single sample
need not equal the new 99–101-s mean. B0b's last-30-s trend is a different
observer from these fixed snapshots and paired changes.

These are no-fit descriptions with 1,000 shared whole-brew resamples, not a
located peak or simultaneous timing threshold. The onset clock differs from
Visualizer's first reported weight >1 g. Time matters here, but the decline
already overlaps Visualizer's later scoring period on the onset clock; time
alone does not explain the corpus difference. Pressure node, coffee, machine
and brew style also differ. No common mechanism, compaction or validation claim.
