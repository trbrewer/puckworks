# Methods, interpretation and reproducibility

The owner approved the amended [one-page plan](PLAN.md) before execution. This is a retrospective, target-exposed scientific screen, using the existing Waszkiewicz cohort rather than an untouched validation set. It changes no production component, registry status, chemistry parameter or form-A coefficient. ## Fixed predictor and numerical application

The [1D column](../../../puckworks/analysis/wetting_screen_001.py) implements the R0 branches in EWP's [espressoWholePullFoam.C at 67bead2](https://github.com/trbrewer/espresso-whole-pull/blob/67bead2a25bd7e61c16c4fe2c104d885d8d26dd9/solver/espressoWholePullFoam/espressoWholePullFoam.C). The configuration is authenticated against each of the 102 retained primary A0/A1 cases. Geometry: radius 0.029 m, depth 0.009011660896432553 m, porosity 0.4; liquid density 965 kg/m³, viscosity 0.000315 Pa·s. Extractable inventory is 20 g × 0.28 = 5.6 g. Release rate 0.15/s, capacity 180 kg/m³ and diffusivity 10⁻⁹ m²/s remain R0 engineering assumptions, not fitted chemistry or independent real-coffee validation.

During filling, the native branch applies local release and dissolved storage without advective or diffusive transport. The saturation profile follows the moving front with the native one-cell smoothing length, L/256. At full saturation, backward-Euler upwind transport and diffusion carry dissolved material to the outlet; the inlet has zero concentration and the outlet zero concentration gradient. Release is explicit, capacity limited, and debited from each cell's remaining inventory. The column retains both dissolved storage and remaining inventory through breakthrough. W1's sharp-front trajectory is the chosen phenomenological trajectory in the plan; smoothing is the same numerical representation as R0. Z/zero-delay W1 starts fully wet with fresh solid inventory and zero dissolved solute. Neither chemistry nor viscosity feeds back into form-A flow.

The native-parity calculation uses 256 cells and EWP's 0.005-s explicit hydraulic update, including no outflow in the step that completes saturation. The search uses the same 256-cell R0 transport, a 0.02-s step, an inserted exact breakthrough event, and a second-order liquid-mass update. Frozen parameters are then evaluated at 0.005 s for reported predictions. [NUMERICAL_CHECKS.csv](NUMERICAL_CHECKS.csv) and [NUMERICAL_SCORES.csv](NUMERICAL_SCORES.csv) disclose the changes without introducing a scientific acceptance threshold. This is verification of the native discretization and time-step sensitivity, not proof of spatial continuum convergence. Across all 395 frozen case pairs, the largest 0.005-minus-0.02-s endpoint change is 0.000323 g/s and the largest detector-onset change is 0.1 s; no detection/non-detection changes. The largest endpoint difference is W2T history 4-5, where shifted flow is interpolated between bed-time samples. W2T endpoint RMSE changes by −0.0000103 g/s and onset-bound RMSE by +0.000524 s; no parameters are refitted. Conservation includes remaining solid, stored dissolved mass, cup solute and diffusive loss to the zero-concentration inlet. No concentration is clipped to the capacity.

## Fold isolation and search

The saved ordinary-LOCO [D0 tuples](../PRESSURE_PEAK_STAGE_D0/RESULT_TABLE.csv) supply a, Pstar, n, Pd and Mc. In outer fold h, every training and held-out brew uses theta_h. The training set excludes all brews at h. A separate DESCRIPTIVE_ALL tuple and all-brew fit supply descriptive curves only. No observed mass, onset or TDS enters the predictor; observed onset enters the training objective and subsequent scoring only. The objective is the equally weighted mean of within-condition mean squared onset errors. This differs from the inherited reporting score, which averages within-condition RMSEs.

The search enumerates the approved 9-point logarithmic k_w0/Pw axes and 21-point linear delay axis, then runs bounded Powell from the five best distinct grid points. Coordinates are log(k_w0), log(Pw) and delay/100 s. The best sampled objective is retained, including grid points; Powell cannot replace it with a worse result. Parameters and domains remain those in the plan. W2 is primary; W2T is the predeclared three-parameter observation diagnostic and explicit exception to the two-parameter limit. There are exactly 36 fits. [FITS.csv](FITS.csv) records equal sampled minima, their parameter ranges, exact boundary minima, distances to bounds and optimizer work. Ranges across equal sampled minima describe non-identification at the detector's sampled resolution; they are neither confidence intervals nor proof that all equivalent solutions have been found. Full trial histories, fit hashes and frozen prediction arrays remain external.

Z is evaluated and reported as an ungraded zero-delay diagnostic, not an eligible candidate. W0 is the unchanged reference. Neither has fitted wetting parameters.

## Observation contract, censoring and scoring

Pressure histories and timestamps keep B0c's stable-pressure origin. The fresh model cup has zero mass on the −2-to-0-s baseline interval. Beverage mass is B = outlet liquid mass + outlet solute mass. It is resampled onto the original observation timestamps in the fixed 0–100-s contract, before differentiation with `numpy.gradient` and the 31-sample, order-1 Savitzky–Golay filter. The inherited `pressure_peak_stage_b0.sustained_onset` uses strict mass >0.5 g and flow >0.1 g/s continuously for at least 2 s, reporting the interval start. At this horizon B0c's post-100-s terminal exclusion has no additional samples to exclude. No outflow is an absence of a TDS measurement, not zero TDS.

W2T shifts both B and scored liquid flow by t_s; values before the delayed trace begins are zero. Bed pressure, breakthrough and recovery retain their original times. [W2T_BED_DIAGNOSTIC.csv](W2T_BED_DIAGNOSTIC.csv) gives unshifted bed-flow scores separately and without grading. Scored flow is liquid ρQ, whereas measured flow is scale-derived beverage flow; [FLOW_BY_BREW.csv](FLOW_BY_BREW.csv) also gives the ungraded endpoint beverage-minus-liquid flow difference.

For valid non-detection, c_i is the latest original sample t_j with T_last − t_j ≥2 s. Its squared-loss contribution is max(0, c_i − observed onset)². NaN from the detector is a non-detection, not a dropped brew. Missing or corrupt prediction coverage is unavailable, never a shorter censor horizon; the predictor spans the full contract. Front censoring at 100 s is separate. Medians use all intended brews; if censoring prevents identification, the reported value is a lower bound, never a detected-only median. [ONSET_BY_BREW.csv](ONSET_BY_BREW.csv) contains individual bounds; [ONSET_BY_PRESSURE.csv](ONSET_BY_PRESSURE.csv) contains all eleven conditions and detection counts.

EWP native verification replays its retained common-support contract, fixed before the Python prediction. One native trace, A1 history 9-1, ends at 99.9999999999815 s while its original source has a 100-s sample. Its full-contract native comparison is explicitly unavailable; the retained-support diagnostic excludes that unsupported sample without extending mass. Screen predictions still span 100 s, use the full source contract, and keep all 56 brews. The three inherited A1 concentration-bound qualification failures (1-2, 2-3, 2-5) remain flagged verification evidence; none is removed from hydraulic or detector comparisons. Earlier EWP evidence is unchanged.

Endpoint, primary onset+2-s and companion ≥5-s flow masks are copied from FORM_A's authenticated source inputs. Scores are the mean of eleven within-condition RMSEs. Paired differences use the same 1,000 condition-then-whole-brew resamples, seed 20261103, for both operands; they are never differences of marginal intervals. All intervals are **conditional on fitted wetting parameters and transferred form-A tuples**. Differences involving censored onset scores are differences of computed bounds, not bounds on the difference of unknown true RMSEs. Intervals on censored scores are intervals for the bound. The inherited C0/D0 onset bands are MEETS ≤3.51 s, NEAR ≤4.53 s, otherwise MISSES; a censored bound earns MISSES only above 4.53 s, otherwise NOT_SCORED/unresolved. Flow accuracy retains the inherited paired comparison and ±0.25-g/s equivalence band. No new gates were added.

The ≥9-bar slope is the inherited regression over 9/11/13-bar condition means, using mean endpoint pressure. Individual 1/2/3.5-bar residuals remain in [GRADES.csv](GRADES.csv). D0's 6.00 [5.77, 6.27]-bar capacity maximum has a parameter-refit interval. Each descriptive 100-s flow maximum instead comes from the same 23-pressure sweep, with bracketed three-point quadratic interpolation near its maximum and no statistical interval. W0's native 6.009-bar value is the reference, not a constraint on other candidates. Detector extrema use the sampled grid; edge minima and censor-obscured/unidentified minima are labeled, with no extra sweep. Held-out nominal extrema mix ordinary folds and are reported separately from the single-tuple descriptive sweep.
