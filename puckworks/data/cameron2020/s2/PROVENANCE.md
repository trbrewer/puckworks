# Cameron Figure S2 pressure/tamp data

Stable dataset: **cameron2020/figS2_pressure_tamp**. Sixteen graphical central
summaries (not authenticated shots), 12 recovered displayed bars and 4 missing
extents. Native 6/9-bar labels, grind 1.3/1.5, four common nominal force groups;
actual plotted x coordinates retained. Per-point n, summary/bar statistic and
shot-clock origin unresolved. No pressure conversion or measured puck-pressure law.
