# Telis-Romero 2001 rheology authority

Source: J. Telis-Romero, R. A. F. Cabral, A. L. Gabas, and V. R. N. Telis,
“Rheological properties and fluid dynamics of coffee extract,” *Journal of
Food Process Engineering* 24 (2001) 217–230,
DOI `10.1111/j.1745-4530.2001.tb00542.x`.

The source reports concentric-cylinder rheometry for one industrial
soluble-coffee extract batch over concentration, temperature, and shear-rate
ranges. Water fraction is percent by mass. Table 1 reports Newtonian viscosity
and Table 2 reports power-law consistency and behavior indices. Quantities are
measured table values or source-fitted values; sample-level replicate structure
is not published and the reported uncertainty is aggregate fit error.

The article is paywalled. This repository contains only compact normalized
factual transcriptions and fitted-closure parameters already used by the G10
rheology component. It contains no article PDF, figures, or raw rheograms.

Maximum current use:
`RHEOLOGY_OR_VISCOSITY_SOURCE_AUTHORITY_FOR_BOUNDED_FLUID_PROPERTY_SENSITIVITY`.
The industrial extract is not fresh espresso liquor. This authority does not
establish coffee-specific production viscosity validation or direct EWP
rheology validation.
